# Round 1 Revision Notes

## Changes Applied

- Tightened the contribution statement around STRIDE as an execution wrapper rather than a full replacement for the VLN policy.
- Added a metric definition section covering safe success, unsafe, timeout, intervention rate, and mean final distance.
- Reworded all external comparisons as adapted baselines and explicitly stated that they are not official reproductions.
- Added cautionary language that the unsafe counts are evaluator-observed and do not constitute a formal safety proof.
- Added figure captions that identify schematics as generated and not camera frames.
- Added manifest provenance for source metrics, official template files, and figure generation.

## Remaining Risk After Round 1

The evaluation still depends on a modest local locked run. A submission version would benefit from more held-out splits, confidence intervals, and hardware validation.
